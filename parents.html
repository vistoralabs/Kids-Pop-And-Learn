Parent & Child Safety

We aim to keep the experience simple for children and transparent for parents.

About the app

Kids Pop & Learn is an educational game focused on simple interactive activities such as colors, letters, numbers, animals, shapes, fruits, vehicles and matching.

Privacy by design

The app is designed to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information from children. The core experience is not intended to require a child to create an account or provide personal details.

Advertising

If advertising is enabled, it is intended to be configured for a child-directed experience in accordance with applicable Google Play and Google AdMob requirements. Ads should never be presented in a deceptive way or in a way that encourages children to click them.

Parent settings

Parent-focused areas may provide access to sound controls, progress information, privacy information and advertising/premium options. The exact settings can change as the app develops.

Questions or concerns

If you have a privacy or child-safety concern, please use the support contact listed on the Contact page.

Important: This page describes the intended design of the app. The final privacy practices must be kept consistent with the actual released app and its third-party SDK configuration.
